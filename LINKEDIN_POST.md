# LinkedIn launch post

🚀 **Introducing Solar System Explorer — an independent engineering project.**

I built an interactive 3D solar system visualisation to explore planetary motion and the engineering ideas behind future space-resource utilisation.

The project includes:
- Animated orbits for the eight planets.
- An illustrative asteroid belt.
- Click-to-focus camera movement.
- Adjustable simulation speed, pause controls, labels and orbital trails.
- Planet information panels.
- A resource explorer covering water ice, iron-nickel, silicates and other materials that may be relevant to future space infrastructure.

I also included a simplified hypothetical asteroid-mining workflow: prospecting, rendezvous, extraction and processing, then using resources in space.

The simulation is intentionally educational rather than scientifically precise. Planetary scales and orbital rates are stylised, and the resource section distinguishes possible materials from proven, economically recoverable deposits.

**Tech used:** JavaScript, Three.js, HTML and CSS.

This project gave me an opportunity to work with 3D scene construction, animation loops, camera controls, structured data and interactive UI design — while exploring a subject that strongly interests me: the future of space engineering.

I'm continuing to improve it and would appreciate constructive feedback.
